# BUSINESS.md — Strategy & Operations

## Model

Ricardo operates a dual-track model:
- **Freelance:** Client projects (web dev, full-stack, AI features) — immediate cash flow
- **Products:** Own SaaS / tools — long-term leverage and recurring revenue

Priority: freelance funds products. Products build independence.

---

## Freelance

### Positioning
- Full-stack developer (Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, AI integrations)
- Bilingual FR/EN — serves French and international clients
- Differentiator: AI-native development + clean architecture + fast delivery

### Ideal Client Profile
- FR: PMEs, startups, agences who need a senior dev without the full-time cost
- EN: Remote-first startups needing Next.js / AI feature work

### Deliverables
- Web apps, dashboards, landing pages, admin panels
- AI feature integration (chatbots, content generation, RAG pipelines)
- API development and third-party integrations

### Communication Style
- Proposals: clear scope, fixed price or day rate, defined deliverables
- Client updates: weekly async (written), real-time only for blockers
- Language: French with FR clients, English with EN clients

## Operational Defaults

- **Pricing:** Never under-price. Anchor high, justify with quality and speed.
- **Contracts:** Always written, always scoped. No verbal agreements.
- **Payments:** 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. Net 0 for recurring.
- **Tools:** Revolut for invoicing (international), Boursorama for FR tax buffer.
- **Legal:** Auto-entrepreneur or SASU depending on annual volume threshold.

## Agent Instructions

When helping with business tasks:
- Draft proposals in the client's language (FR or EN)
- Keep scope tight — avoid feature creep in client specs
- Flag scope changes that should trigger a change order
- Prioritize tasks that generate cash flow or unblock product delivery
