# LEARNING.md — Lifelong Learning & Personal Growth

## Philosophy

Learning is compounding. Consistency beats intensity.
Three pillars: **Technical mastery** (dev), **Financial intelligence** (BTC/investing), **Physical & mental base** (callisthenics, music, mindfulness).

---

- **Current focus:** AI SDK integration, RAG pipelines, agent orchestration
- **Tools:** Claude Code, OpenClaw, Vercel AI SDK

### 2. Financial Intelligence

- **Method:** Weekly review of positions and macro signals; no daily noise

### 4. Physical — Callisthenics

### 5. Music — Acoustic Guitar
- **Tool:** Ultimate Guitar + Metronome app
- **Method:** 15–20 min daily practice, song-based learning
- **Goal:** Consistent technique, new song per month

### 6. Mindfulness & Reflection
- **Tool:** Insight Timer
- **Method:** Morning silence or short meditation (5–10 min)
- **Goal:** Mental clarity before deep work sessions

## Learning System

### Daily (5 min max)
- **Morning:** 1 learning micro-moment (tech tip, financial signal, or callisthenics drill)
- **Evening:** 1-sentence journal entry — "What did I learn or do today that compounds?"

### Weekly (Sunday or Monday)
- Review learning log — what stuck, what to drill again
- Promote durable insights to `MEMORY.md`
- Identify 1 thing to go deeper on next week

### Spaced Repetition
- Use Anki for: vocabulary, code patterns, financial concepts, historical facts
- Review deck: minimum 3×/week

## Agent Instructions

When helping with learning tasks:
- Surface one actionable insight, not a wall of information
- Frame tech concepts with a working code example when possible
- For finance: signal over noise — macro context only when materially relevant
- For journaling prompts: keep them short, specific, and forward-looking
- Never replace the user's own reflection — prompt and scaffold, don't answer for them
