# MARKETING.md — SEO, GEO & Content Strategy

## Principles

- **SEO:** Rank in search engines via keyword-targeted, structured content.
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization):** Be cited in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude). Requires authoritative, entity-rich, fact-dense writing.
- **Content:** One piece of long-form content per week minimum. Repurpose across channels.
- **Language:** FR content for FR projects; EN content for EN/international projects.

## Content Calendar Framework

### Weekly cadence
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Monday | Identify 1 keyword target for the week |
| Tuesday | Draft long-form article (800–1500 words) |
| Thursday | Publish + add internal links + schema markup |
| Friday | Distribute: LinkedIn (EN), Twitter/X, newsletter |

### Content formats (GEO-optimized)
1. **FAQ pages** — direct answers to questions AI models surface (highest citation rate)
2. **Comparison articles** — "X vs Y" format (high search intent + AI citation)
3. **How-to guides** — step-by-step with numbered lists (structured = machine-readable)
4. **Case studies** — measurable outcomes (builds authority and trust signals)
5. **Glossaries** — define key terms in your niche (long-tail + GEO gold)

---

## GEO Checklist (apply to every published page)

- [ ] Answer a question directly in the first paragraph (inverted pyramid)
- [ ] Include structured data (JSON-LD schema)
- [ ] Use H2/H3 headers that match common question formats
- [ ] Cite primary sources (official docs, studies, authoritative sites)
- [ ] Include entity mentions (people, places, brands) that AI models recognize
- [ ] Keep sentences under 25 words for machine parsing
- [ ] Add a FAQ section at the bottom of every article
